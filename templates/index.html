Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding appointments, billing, and consultations.

How do I book an appointment with a specialist?
Simply register or sign in to your Patient Portal, navigate to "Book Appointment", select your doctor and desired time slot, and click Confirm. Your appointment token will be generated instantly.
Are emergency and ICU services available 24/7?
Yes, our Emergency and Trauma Care Unit is operational 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with on-call emergency physicians, intensive care specialists, and dedicated ambulance response.
How do I pay my hospital consultation or treatment bills?
You can pay online via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), Debit/Credit Cards through our secure cashless gateway in the Patient Portal, or at the hospital billing counter.
Where can I view my prescriptions and doctor diagnosis?
Once your doctor completes your consultation and issues an electronic prescription, it is permanently saved in your Patient Health Portal records for immediate access.
